## Defects found & fixed

### P2 — Rainbow "soap-bubble" moiré on all water (FIXED)

- Symptom: every water cell covered by translucent rainbow-edged
  bubbles from top-down/far views (worst in `topdown.png`).
- Root cause: `_CausticsChromance 0.754` — chromatic voronoi caustics
  on the rendered sand bed, seen through the transparent Nintendo water;
  `_RefractionChromaticAberration 0.042` added RGB fringing.
- Fix in `StylizedWater3_NintendoStyle.mat`: caustics off
  (keyword + float), CA→0, surface-foam-single off, translucency off,
  distance normals off, env reflections off, `_SunReflectionStrength` 0.
- Verified: `topdown.png` / `detail_confluence.png` / `edge_s.png` after
  — clean navy water, bed gradient still readable.
- Commit: `8a6fe50d fix(water): kill rainbow moire on water`.

### P2 — Giant sedge meshes spanning cells and off-map (FIXED)

- Symptom: thin black/white blades radiating off the map rim
  (`game_orbit_e`, `game_shore`); oob dump showed `veg-sedge-*` meshes
  with vertex spans up to 9×6 cells, Y up to 7.
- Root cause: `MoyvaVegetationAssetBuilder.BuildSedgeMesh` bend
  `lean * 0.01f * bh * 60f` → ~7-unit tip drift per blade.
- Fix: `tan(leanDeg) * bh` — mesh bounds 6.8×13.8 → 0.21×0.42 /
  8.7×7.8 → 0.27×0.23.
- Verified: oob prop entries **128 → 39**; remaining = minor tree/stump
  rim overhangs ≤1.2 u (P3, canopies at borders).
- Also: `BuildAll()` chained into `MoyvaAtlasPackImporter.Import()` —
  was menu-only, so generated meshes could silently drift again.
- Commit: `65fc3f9c fix(vegetation): sedge blades spanned cells`.
